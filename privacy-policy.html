Privacy Policy for Tick Tock Time School

Last updated: [DATE]

Tick Tock Time School ("the App") is operated by Anand Popat ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App uses, how it is used, and how you can control it.

We built this App for children, and we take children's privacy seriously. The App is designed to collect as little information as possible, and to process it only temporarily where needed to deliver the App's functionality. Information entered into the App is not stored on any server we control (see Section 1 for the one exception, where a name is briefly processed to generate audio, and Section 6 for platform-level diagnostics).

1. Information the App Uses

When a player sets up their profile, the App may ask for:

  • First name or nickname (optional — can be left blank)
  • Age (optional — can be skipped)
  • Gender (optional)
  • A chosen animal character

This information is used only to personalize the player's experience within the App (for example, generating a spoken voice line using the player's name). None of this information is stored on any server we control, or shared with any third party for marketing, advertising, or analytics purposes.

If a name is entered, it is temporarily transmitted to our audio-generation service solely to create a personalized voice clip. That voice clip is then cached (saved) directly on the player's own device. The name itself is used only for that moment of processing, is not retained by the audio-generation service afterward, and is not stored anywhere outside the device.

If no name is entered, the App uses a generic, non-personalized voice instead.

Our audio-generation service keeps basic technical logs confirming that a request was received and a response was sent (for example, for troubleshooting purposes). These logs do not contain the player's name or any other personal information.

2. Game Progress and Scores

Level progress and Hall of Fame scores are saved only on the player's own device. This information is not uploaded to, or stored on, any server we control.

3. In-App Purchases

The App offers an optional in-app purchase to unlock additional levels. Purchases are processed entirely through Apple's App Store or Google Play, as applicable. We do not receive or store any payment information; this is handled directly by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies.

To help ensure purchases are made with a parent or guardian's permission, the App includes a "parental gate" before any purchase can be made.

4. Children's Privacy

This App is designed with young children in mind, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is described in this policy. As described in Section 1, a name is only ever processed momentarily to generate audio, and is not stored by us. We do not use the App to serve targeted advertising, and we do not knowingly allow any third party to collect personal information through the App.

5. Deleting Your Data

Since all personal information (name, cached audio, and scores) is stored only on your device, you can delete it at any time:

  • Use the Delete Profile button within the App, which removes cached audio and Hall of Fame scores from your device, or
  • Uninstall the App, which removes all locally stored data.

6. Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services to function:

  • Apple App Store / Google Play — for app distribution and in-app purchases
  • [Runpod / audio generation service] — used only momentarily to generate a personalized voice clip from a name you provide, if entered; the resulting audio is stored only on your device

These services operate under their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

We do not add any analytics or crash-reporting tools to the App ourselves. On Android devices, Google may automatically collect basic crash and "Application Not Responding" (ANR) diagnostics directly through the Android platform (via Android vitals), according to the privacy settings you have chosen on your device. We may use reports made available to us through Google Play, based on this platform-level diagnostic data, to help us identify and fix stability issues. This collection is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
